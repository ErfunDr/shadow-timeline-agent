**Main Topic:** FTX, a cryptocurrency exchange, faces collapse and bankruptcy due to liquidity crisis and mismanagement of customer funds.

**Important Events:**

1. **November 2022**: Reports emerge showing problems in Alameda Research's balance sheet, indicating potential issues with the company's financial management.
2. **Late November/Early December 2022**: Users start withdrawing funds from FTX, exacerbating the liquidity crisis.
3. **December 2022**: FTX files for bankruptcy, marking a significant turning point in the company's downfall.
4. **December 2022**: Sam Bankman-Fried resigns as CEO of FTX, following the filing for bankruptcy.

**Mystery Angle:**

* The extent to which Alameda Research's balance sheet issues were related to its overall financial health and whether they contributed directly to FTX's collapse is unclear.
* There may be more information about the specific events leading up to the withdrawal of funds by users and how it was handled by FTX.
