FTX was one of the largest cryptocurrency exchanges in the world.

In early November 2022, reports emerged about Alameda Research's balance sheet. The report suggested that a large part of Alameda's assets were connected to FTT, the token created by FTX.

After these reports, confidence in FTX started to collapse.

Many users began withdrawing their funds from the exchange.

FTX faced a liquidity crisis because it could not process all withdrawal requests.

Binance first announced that it might acquire FTX, but later backed out of the deal.

Soon after, FTX filed for bankruptcy.

Sam Bankman-Fried resigned as CEO.

Investigations later focused on whether customer funds had been misused and how closely FTX and Alameda Research were connected.
